Furnace Master

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Scope and developer

This policy explains how Furnace Master handles information. The developer is Yang Jinjing. Contact: yangjinjingGZ@163.com. The game is intended for players aged 9 and above, including children, teenagers and adults.

Main levels and daily challenges can be played offline. The game currently has no player accounts, developer-operated cloud saves, online rankings or in-app purchases. Rankings show records from this device.

2. Records on your device

The app stores completed levels, music, sound and vibration settings, and daily challenge dates, puzzle identifiers, timer start and finish times, elapsed time, restart and menu-return counts and previous records.

Daily scheduling stores the date, cycle, index and puzzle order to avoid repeating puzzles within a cycle. These records support progress, local rankings and settings. They are not currently uploaded to a developer-operated game server.

The advertising age choice stores only an age group to control ad features. It does not ask for a real name, full birth date or identity document. This choice is stored in app data and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

3. Advertising and privacy services

The Android app includes Google advertising and privacy components. Ads load only where enabled, after the player chooses to watch and the component permits a request. Normal play does not require ads. The test build uses official test ads; production ads are not configured.

When these services are enabled, Google components may collect and transmit to Google IP addresses and inferred approximate location, ad and app interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers where permitted by system settings and version configuration, such as advertising or app-set identifiers, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The game does not request precise location permission.

The privacy component manages applicable messages and choices. The game does not separately store consent strings. Google handles its data under https://policies.google.com/privacy . Completing a privacy form does not award an in-game reward.

4. Children and age choices

The audience includes ages 9–12. Children may read this policy with a parent or guardian. Normal offline play does not require personal information or an account. The game has no player chat or user-content uploads.

Under the current Android ad safeguard, no age choice or a choice below age 18 prevents advertising privacy requests and rewarded ad loading. Players who choose an age group below 18 can watch main-level demos free. Unknown-age players first choose a group without a preselected answer. Cancelling leaves normal play available but does not unlock a demo. Only a confirmed age of 18 or above permits the existing privacy and rewarded-ad flow. Daily challenges do not offer solution demos.

In the Android game, you can change the choice through Advertising age settings in the privacy policy window. An age choice is not proof of parental consent. Ads for children are not enabled. Any future change requires additional safeguards, an updated policy and further verification.

5. System backup and retention

Game records are mainly stored in device app data. Android backup is currently allowed, so records may be backed up or transferred according to your device, system, account and backup settings. System and manufacturer services manage their own data handling.

Local game records do not expire automatically. They remain until app data is cleared or otherwise deleted. Uninstalling normally removes device app data, but may leave system backups that can be restored after reinstalling.

Google and backup services manage retention under their own policies and settings. The developer cannot directly delete all records held by third parties.

6. Deletion and privacy choices

You can clear the game’s app data in Android app settings. This deletes local progress, scores and settings; consider whether you wish to keep them first. Manage backup deletion or disabling through your system or backup service.

Reset settings only restores music, sound and vibration; it does not erase progress or scores. When required by Google’s privacy component, Settings shows Privacy options for the choices that the component provides.

Use system features to reset or delete advertising identifiers. For information you voluntarily send to the developer, contact support to request access, correction or deletion. Necessary details may be checked to handle the request.

7. Support email

If you email support, the developer receives your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. These are used to answer questions, handle requests and meet necessary legal obligations. Information no longer needed is deleted. The email provider also handles email under its own policies.

Children are encouraged to ask a parent or guardian to contact support. Do not send identity documents, payment credentials or unrelated sensitive information. The developer does not request these through the game.

8. Updates and contact

We update this policy and its date when features or data handling change. New processing or matters requiring consent will be explained or accompanied by choices where applicable.
